# Design references

Kage's launcher and expressions were independently implemented for LVGL while
studying these MIT-licensed/open design resources:

- Bencho Magnetic Select — <https://bencho.dev/>
- SmoothUI AI Orb Face — <https://smoothui.dev/docs/components/ai-orb-face>
- thinking-orbs — <https://github.com/Jakubantalik/thinking-orbs>

No React, Motion, GSAP, browser canvas or source images from those projects are
embedded in the firmware.
